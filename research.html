Graduate study

I'm applying to Master's programs in computer science and machine learning systems. My published work is applied ML in two domains with the same instinct behind both: take a physical system that's slow or expensive to measure, and learn a model of it good enough to search. Seven peer-reviewed papers, four of them Q1, 75+ citations, one as first author.

What I want next is the systems side of ML rather than the applications side. Efficient inference and serving, multi-agent architectures, and the memory and scheduling problems that appear when models meet real hardware. That's not a new interest. I've contributed to a KAIST lab's LLM-serving simulator, and my extension of an ICML Spotlight paper was adopted into the authors' own repo. My electrical engineering training is why I find these problems legible.
